EdgeFace's licence is split, and the two halves do not say the same thing.
This file records both, because `edgeface_xs_gamma_06.onnx` beside it is
derived from the weights and governed by whichever of the two applies.

Declared id: CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 — the stricter of the two, so what `GET /models`
shows is the obligation a reader has to honour.

Weights: the official Idiap model cards, huggingface.co/idiap/EdgeFace-XS-GAMMA
and huggingface.co/idiap/EdgeFace-S-GAMMA, both declare

  license: cc-by-nc-sa-4.0

which permits use for NonCommercial purposes only. meco produces marketing
images, which is commercial by intention, so the two do not sit comfortably
together. That is an accepted risk, not an oversight: ADR-0003, Decision 1
records it, bounds it, and names the trigger that reopens it — another
operator, or a paid tier. The resolution path is to ask Idiap directly, with
ONNX Model Zoo ArcFace R100 (Apache-2.0) as the licence-clean fallback.

Provenance of the file in this folder: exported from the pinned PyTorch
checkpoint `edgeface_xs_gamma_06.pt` (sha256
5ae7504cd9aee0a5d52c2115fd2eb66b0985dd1730f40134b5854e0cb658ce16) of
github.com/otroshi/edgeface at commit
ce86851cfc37979a9cd2558598d0e9bc592cbba3, torch 2.14.0, ONNX opset 17. Its own
sha256 is pinned in `weights.sha256` beside it.
